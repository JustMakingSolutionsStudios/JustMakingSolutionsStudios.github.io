About JMStudios
Just Making Solutions, Studios

Founded in 2012 by a small group of ambitious college students with a shared passion for technology and innovation to find solutions to our surrounding communities issues, our agency has since evolved into a lean, professional software team. With combined decades of experience under our belts, we've honed our skills and expanded our knowledge, growing from enthusiastic students into seasoned professionals. Today, we boast a portfolio enriched with collaborations alongside dozens of brands, each project a testament to our commitment to excellence and our journey from a modest start-up to a distinguished name in the software industry.

At the heart of our agency is a relentless pursuit of quality, modernity, and client satisfaction. We've witnessed firsthand the industry's challenges, particularly the prevalence of bug-riddled products that compromise brand integrity and waste invaluable time. This observation spurred our founding and continues to drive our mission: to deliver exceptional, reliable software solutions tailored to our clients' unique needs. Through transparent communication, meticulous attention to detail, and an unwavering dedication to deadlines, we don't just meet expectations—we set new benchmarks for what it means to deliver high-quality, impactful software solutions.
